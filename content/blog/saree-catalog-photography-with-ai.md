A saree is six yards of fabric with several different designs on it: the body, the border, the pallu and often a blouse piece. A front and a back photo cannot show all of that, so a saree shoot asks for more.

## The four required photos

Youthnic AI Studio will not start a saree shoot until these four are uploaded.

1. **Full front.** The saree draped and worn, head to toe.
2. **Back drape.** The same drape from behind, so the fall of the pallu over the shoulder is on record.
3. **Pallu, fully spread.** Open the pallu flat and photograph the whole of it. The pallu usually carries the richest work, and a folded pallu hides most of it.
4. **Body fabric detail.** A close photo of the main body: the weave, the butti, the print repeat.

Two more are recommended: the **border and tassels**, and the **blouse**.

## Why the spread pallu matters most

In a draped photo the pallu is pleated over the shoulder, and only a narrow strip is visible. If that strip is all the studio has, the rest of the pallu in the finished frames is an estimate. Spread flat, the full design is read from your photo and carried into the frames.

## How to take each photo

- Use daylight and the same spot for all four, so colours agree.
- For the spread pallu, lay it on a clean plain surface and shoot from directly above.
- For the body detail, fill the frame with fabric and keep the phone steady so the weave is sharp.
- Keep zari and metallic thread out of direct sun; glare hides the pattern.
- Do not use a filter. Silk colours shift easily.

## What the shoot delivers

The same six frames as any other shoot: full front, a turn, the back, a signature pose, face and details, and a product highlight. For a saree, the highlight is usually the pallu or the border.

Posture is handled with care. A saree is never shot reclining, because a reclining pose pulls the drape apart and stops the frame from showing the product.

## Common problems, and the photo that prevents each

- **The pallu design looks simplified.** Add or retake the fully spread pallu.
- **The border is the wrong width.** Add a border close-up with a little of the body for scale.
- **The colour is off.** Retake all photos in the same daylight, without a filter.
- **The blouse does not match.** Upload the blouse or blouse piece separately.

## Before you upload

Lay the four photos side by side and ask: could someone who has never seen this saree draw it from these? If yes, the shoot has what it needs.
