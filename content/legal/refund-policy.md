This policy covers the free trial and the prepaid packs for Youthnic AI Studio.

## The free trial

The trial is free. There is nothing to refund.

## How packs work

A pack is a prepaid number of SKUs. One SKU is one product shot as a full six-frame set. A shoot uses one SKU from your balance when it starts.

## If a shoot fails

If a shoot fails to start, the SKU is returned to your balance automatically. You do not need to ask.

If a shoot starts and then fails before all six frames are delivered, write to us with the SKU code and we will review it.

## If a frame is wrong

Each frame is checked against your product photos. A frame that does not pass is generated again within the same shoot. If a delivered frame still does not match your product, write to us with the SKU code and the frame, and we will look at it.

## Refunds for unused SKUs

If you bought a pack and have not used it, write to us within 7 days of the purchase and we will refund the unused SKUs. SKUs that have already been used for a shoot are not refundable.

## How to ask for a refund

Write to pawan@youthnic.shop from the email address on your account, with:

- your store name;
- the pack you bought and the date;
- your payment reference.

We reply within 3 working days. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 7 working days.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. The policy in force when you bought a pack applies to that pack.
