This policy explains what Youthnic AI Studio ("we", "the studio") collects when you use the service, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it. Youthnic is based in Surat, Gujarat, India.

## What we collect

- **Account details.** Your email address, your store or brand name, and a password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are never visible to us.
- **What you upload.** Product photos, and any model face or style reference photos you choose to add, together with the SKU codes and notes you type.
- **What the studio produces.** The analysis of each garment, the pose plan, the generated images and every earlier version of them.
- **Usage records.** Which shoots were run, when, their status and their cost, so that your history, credits and invoices are correct.
- **Technical data.** Error reports and basic request logs, used to keep the service working.

We do not ask for payment card details inside the app.

## Why we use it

- To create your account and keep it secure.
- To run the shoots you start and show you the results.
- To keep your history so you can download or regenerate images later.
- To answer you when you write to us.
- To find and fix faults.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your product photos or generated images to advertise to other people without your permission.

## Who processes it for us

We use other companies to run the service. They process data only to provide their part of it:

- A sign-in provider, for accounts and sessions.
- Cloud storage and database providers, for your photos, images and records.
- AI model providers, which receive your uploaded photos and the instructions for a shoot in order to analyse the garment and generate the images.
- An error monitoring provider.

Some of these providers store or process data outside India.

## Photos of people

If you upload a photo that shows a person, such as a model face or a style reference, you confirm that you have the right to use that photo for this purpose. Do not upload a photo of someone without their permission.

## How long we keep it

We keep your shoots and their versions while your account is open, so that paid work is never lost. When you ask us to close your account, we delete your account details, uploads and generated images, except records we must keep by law, such as payment records.

## Your choices

You can ask us to:

- show you the data we hold about your account;
- correct your account details;
- delete a shoot, or your whole account.

Write to pawan@youthnic.shop from the email address on your account.

## Security

Each store's data is kept separate from every other store's. Sessions end after 24 hours and you are asked to sign in again. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use elsewhere.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so on this page and update the date above.
