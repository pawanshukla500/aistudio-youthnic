# Quality Validation

To ensure no defective images reach your product listings, Youthnic AI Studio checks generated photos in two layers:

- the **product consistency check**, which runs on every pose of every shoot;
- full **automatic QA**, which runs only when you switch it on for the shoot or tick it when regenerating a pose.

## Product Consistency Check (Always On)

Before a pose is accepted, it is compared with your product photos on five attributes: **shape**, **colour**, **material**, **logo** and **details** (embroidery, print, trims and their placement). Logo is skipped for products without one.

- Any attribute below the pass mark fails the check. The image is saved to the rejected archive instead of being deleted (unless storage is unavailable) and the pose is regenerated within the normal retry limit, with the failed attributes named in the retry. If the last attempt fails only this check (automatic QA, when on, passed it), that image is kept and marked for human review instead of being discarded. An image that automatic QA also rejects follows QA's own rules for the last attempt.
- If the check cannot run (for example a model outage), the image is delivered and marked unverified; the check is shown as unavailable.
- When full automatic QA is on, the five scores are taken from the QA review, so the check is not run twice.

In **History**, open a pose to see **Product consistency check**: Passed, Failed or Unavailable, with the five scores.

## Full Automatic QA (Opt-In)

When automatic QA is on, each photo also gets the multi-point review below before it is marked completed.

```mermaid
graph TD
    A[Rendered 4K Image] --> B[Automated QA Vision Pipeline]
    B --> C[Anatomical Coherence Check]
    B --> D[Garment Fidelity & Pattern Verification]
    B --> E[Colorimetry Delta-E Test]
    B --> F[Artifact & Edge Sharpness Analysis]
    C & D & E & F --> G{Overall Score >= 85?}
    G -- Yes --> H[Mark Approved / Green Badge]
    G -- No --> I[Auto-Retry or Flag for Manual Review]
```

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="1. Anatomical Coherence (Weight: 30%)" icon="user-check">
    Evaluates human anatomy for natural poise and structural correctness:
    - **Hand & Finger Count**: Confirms exactly 5 fingers per hand, natural joint flexion, and realistic palm curvature.
    - **Facial Symmetry**: Evaluates iris alignment, natural eyelid crease, lip shape, and jawline definition.
    - **Limb Geometry**: Verifies natural elbow angles, knee articulation, and foot-to-ground anchoring.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="2. Garment Fidelity & Pattern Matching (Weight: 35%)" icon="shirt">
    Compares the generated garment against the original uploaded reference photos:
    - **Neckline & Placket**: Checks neckline depth, collar sharpness, and button spacing.
    - **Embroidery Motif Preservation**: Verifies floral or geometric embroidery density, border alignment, and zari work.
    - **Silhouette Accuracy**: Ensures an A-line kurti was not rendered as a bodycon dress or loose kaftan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="3. Colorimetry & Delta-E Tolerance (Weight: 20%)" icon="palette">
    Calculates optical color deviation ($\Delta E$) between the reference fabric hex code and the generated garment under studio lighting.
    - $\Delta E < 3.0$: Imperceptible difference (Grade A+).
    - $\Delta E < 6.0$: Commercial acceptable variation under ambient lighting.
    - $\Delta E > 8.0$: Color shift triggered; flagged for automatic re-render.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="4. Edge & Artifact Analysis (Weight: 15%)" icon="sparkles">
    Scans for edge fringing, halo artifacts around the model silhouette, texture smudging, or background distortion.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Score Statuses & Thresholds

| Score Range | Status | Action Taken |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **90 – 100** | <span style={{color: '#10b981', fontWeight: 600}}>Excellent</span> | Automatically approved. Image is ready for instant download or marketplace export. |
| **80 – 89** | <span style={{color: '#3b82f6', fontWeight: 600}}>Good</span> | Approved for catalog use with minor ambient lighting variation. |
| **70 – 79** | <span style={{color: '#f59e0b', fontWeight: 600}}>Needs Review</span> | Flagged with an amber warning icon. Click to inspect before deciding to approve or regenerate. |
| **Below 70** | <span style={{color: '#ef4444', fontWeight: 600}}>Defective</span> | Automatically triggers a backend re-render with adjusted latent seeds (up to 2 automatic retries). If still below threshold, marked failed and credits are refunded. |

## Manual Quality Override

As a merchandiser or creative lead, you always have the final say:
- Click **Approve** to dismiss a "Needs Review" flag.
- Click **Reject & Regenerate** to re-render the pose with custom prompt feedback (e.g. *"Fix right hand finger alignment"*).
