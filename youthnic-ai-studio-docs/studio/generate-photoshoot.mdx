# Generate Photoshoot

Once your reference images are analyzed, creative direction is set, and the five-pose catalog plan is configured, you are ready to render the photoshoot.

---

The pre-flight check validates:
1. **Front Reference Presence**: Ensures at least one valid front image is assigned.
2. **Product Analysis Status**: Verifies that AI analysis has extracted the garment identity.
3. **SKU Uniqueness**: Warns if the SKU has already been generated in the current workspace to avoid overwriting previous assets.
4. **Credit Balance**: Confirms your organization has sufficient credits to fulfill the enabled poses.

## Credit Consumption Rules

| Operation | Credits Consumed | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard 5-Pose Photoshoot** | 5 Credits | 1 credit per enabled pose. |
| **Partial Photoshoot (e.g. 3 Poses)** | 3 Credits | Disabled poses are excluded from billing. |
| **Single Image Regeneration** | 1 Credit | Re-rendering a single pose consumes 1 credit. |
| **Failed Image Generation** | **0 Credits** | If an image fails due to server timeout or fails automated QA checks, the credit is automatically refunded immediately. |

---

## Multi-Provider Orchestration Under the Hood

When generation begins, Youthnic's backend AI router selects the optimal generative model based on the garment complexity and your organization's admin policy:
- **Ultra-Fine Embroidery & Zari**: Routed to high-parameter vision conditioning pipelines with specular texture locks.
- **Complex Drapery (Sarees / Lehengas)**: Dispatched to specialized spatial pose guidance adapters.
- **Concurrency**: All enabled poses render simultaneously in parallel workers, reducing total turnaround time to under 60–90 seconds.
