# Product Identity Profile

The **Product Identity Profile** is the digital blueprint representing your physical garment inside Youthnic AI Studio. Once generated by [AI Product Analysis](/studio/ai-product-analysis), this profile acts as an authoritative constraint across all generated poses, preventing "garment drift" or color shifts.

## Anatomy of the Identity Profile

The profile is divided into four structured sections:

### 1. Optical Colorimetry & Swatches
- **Primary Body Color**: Hex code, color name, and detected undertone (e.g. `#5B1E31` Plum Wine).
- **Secondary / Accent Colors**: Hex codes for dupattas, border trims, or print motifs.
- **Metallic Specular Highlights**: Reflective metallic finish classification (e.g. *Antique Gold Zari*, *Silver Gotapatti*, *Copper Sequins*).

### 2. Textile Physics & Drape
- **Material Construction**: Detected fabric blend (e.g. *80% Mulberry Silk, 20% Georgette*).
- **Weave Density**: Sheer, lightweight, medium-weight, or heavy structured.
- **Surface Texture**: Matte crepe, lustrous satin, granular georgette, ribbed brocade, or crisp organza.

### 3. Structural Geometry
- **Length & Hemline**: Floor-length, ankle-length, knee-length, asymmetrical handkerchief hem.
- **Neckline Front & Rear**: Front depth and shape (V-neck, scoop, boat, keyhole) plus rear cut depth.
- **Sleeve Architecture**: Sleeve cut, cuff finish, armhole fit, and wrist gathers.
- **Slits & Openings**: Center front slit, side kalis, princess seams, or back zipper closure.

### 4. Embellishment & Craft Mapping
- **Placement Regions**: Neckline yoke, sleeve cuffs, hem border, back shoulder, all-over scatter.
- **Technique**: Zardozi, dori work, sequin micro-clusters, thread aari work, foil stamping.

## Technical Profile JSON Export

For enterprise PIM or PLM integration, the Product Identity Profile can be copied directly as structured JSON by clicking the `{}` icon at the top right of the card:

```json
{
  "garment_id": "profile_98741_yn",
  "category": "Ethnic Wear",
  "sub_category": "Anarkali Suit Set",
  "fabric": {
    "type": "Pure Silk Georgette",
    "weight": "Lightweight / Flowing",
    "translucency": 0.15,
    "sheen": "Subtle Satin Luster"
  },
  "palette": {
    "primary_hex": "#5B1E31",
    "accent_hex": "#D4AF37",
    "undertone": "Cool Burgundy"
  },
  "embellishment": {
    "type": "Gotapatti & Zari Borders",
    "coverage_percentage": 25,
    "primary_locations": ["neckline", "sleeves", "hem_border"]
  }
}
```
