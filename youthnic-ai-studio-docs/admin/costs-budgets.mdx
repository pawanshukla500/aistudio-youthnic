# Costs & Budgets Administration

The **Costs & Budgets** console (`/admin/costs-budgets`) provides financial controllers with the tools required to track, predict, and cap spending on AI visual production across all teams.

<Tip>
When a team reaches 100% of its quota, operators are notified to request a budget extension from the administrator. Production does not automatically freeze unless hard enforcement is enabled.
</Tip>

## 2. Automated Credit Auto-Recharge

To prevent overnight catalog batches from stopping midway through execution:

[SCREENSHOT REQUIRED:
Page: Administration (/admin/costs-budgets)
Exact State: Auto-Recharge modal showing toggle active: 'When balance drops below 500 Credits, automatically purchase 2,500 Credits package using corporate card'.
Exact UI Section: Auto-Recharge configuration modal.
Recommended Crop: 550x380
Recommended Dimensions: 550x380
Filename: admin-auto-recharge-modal.webp]

- **Threshold Level**: Trigger purchase when balance falls below X credits (e.g. 500 Credits).
- **Recharge Amount**: Automatically add a pre-approved credit tier.
- **Monthly Limit**: Enforces a maximum number of auto-recharges per billing cycle (e.g. Max 3 times per month).

---

## 3. Invoice History & Tax Invoices

Download official GST/VAT compliant tax invoices for your corporate accounting team:
- Click **Invoices & Receipts** to access itemized monthly PDF statements.
- Includes SKU counts, compute hours, and payment method transaction IDs.
