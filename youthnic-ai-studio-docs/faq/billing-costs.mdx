# Billing & Credits FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How are credits counted?" icon="coins">
    - **One credit is one shoot**: one SKU, delivered as six catalog frames.
    - **Queuing a shoot uses one credit.** If the shoot cannot be queued, the credit is returned automatically.
    - **Product analysis does not use a credit.**
    - Credits apply to seller stores that signed up themselves. An internal workspace has full access and no balance to recharge.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do I get for free when I sign up?" icon="shield">
    A new store gets **one free shoot**. Verify your email first: open the link we send when you sign up, then press **I have verified** in the banner at the top of the app. You can resend the email from the same banner.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I recharge?" icon="credit-card">
    Open **Credits** in the sidebar, choose a pack and send the request. Online payment is not connected yet, so email us from that page to arrange payment. Your shoots are added as soon as the payment is confirmed, and the request shows in your recharge history.

    | Pack | Price |
    | :--- | :--- |
    | 2 SKUs | ₹500 |
    | 11 SKUs | ₹2,000 |
    | 30 SKUs | ₹5,000 |
    | 60 SKUs | ₹10,000 |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change or cancel a recharge request?" icon="arrow-rotate-left">
    Yes, until it is approved. A store has one open request at a time: cancel it on the Credits page, then request another pack.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
