# Billing & Credits FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How are generation credits counted and billed?" icon="coins">
    - **Standard Rate**: Exactly **1 Credit = 1 Generated Image Pose**.
    - **Complete Photoshoot**: A standard 5-pose catalog photoshoot consumes **5 Credits**.
    - **Single Pose Regeneration**: Re-rendering an individual pose consumes **1 Credit**.
    - **AI Product Vision Analysis**: Completely **Free of Charge** (unlimited garment analysis).
    - **4K Lightbox Inspection & Re-Downloads**: Completely **Free of Charge**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my credits if an image generation fails?" icon="arrow-rotate-left">
    **You are never charged for failed generations.** If a GPU worker times out, or if the automated quality assurance engine rejects an image for anatomical anomalies, the credit is immediately and automatically refunded back to your balance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do unused monthly credits roll over?" icon="calendar">
    Yes. Unused credits roll over for up to **90 days** on active subscription plans, ensuring you don't lose value during seasonal sampling down-times.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I enforce hard monthly spending limits on my team?" icon="shield">
    **Yes.** Workspace administrators can allocate dedicated credit quotas to specific department teams (e.g. 2,000 credits for Bridal, 1,000 credits for Western) or set an organization-wide hard spend ceiling in [Costs & Budgets](/admin/costs-budgets).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I configure automatic credit recharge?" icon="credit-card">
    Admins can toggle **Auto-Recharge** in [Costs & Budgets](/admin/costs-budgets). When the balance drops below your chosen safety threshold (e.g. 500 credits), the platform automatically purchases a pre-approved credit pack using your corporate card on file, ensuring overnight batches are never halted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
