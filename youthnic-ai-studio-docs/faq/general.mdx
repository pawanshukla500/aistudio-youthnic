# General FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Youthnic AI Studio?" icon="circle-question">
    Youthnic AI Studio is a specialized AI-powered commercial catalog production platform designed for fashion brands, e-commerce retailers, and catalog agencies. It transforms flat lays, ghost mannequin photos, and fabric swatches into high-fashion 5-pose commercial photoshoots with full editorial control over models, sets, lighting, and styling.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I own full commercial rights to the generated images?" icon="shield-check">
    **Yes, 100%.** You own full commercial rights to all generated photoshoots. You can use them freely on e-commerce marketplaces (Amazon, Myntra, Nykaa, Ajio), digital storefronts (Shopify, WooCommerce), social media advertising, print lookbooks, and outdoor billboards without recurring licensing fees or model royalty obligations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What apparel categories are supported?" icon="shirt">
    Youthnic has specialized support for both Indian ethnic wear and global western fashion:
    - **Indian Ethnic**: Sarees (with pallu/pleat conditioning), Bridal Lehengas, Anarkalis, Salwar Kameez, Kurtis, Sharara Sets, Indo-Western Gowns.
    - **Western Wear**: Dresses (mini, midi, maxi), Co-ord sets, Tops & Blouses, Tailored Blazers, Outerwear, Trousers & Skirts.
    - **Men's & Fusion**: Kurtas, Nehru Jackets, Sherwanis, Western Shirts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How fast is the generation process?" icon="bolt">
    A complete single-product 5-pose commercial photoshoot renders in approximately **45 to 75 seconds**. A batch catalog of 10 colourways (50 total 4K images) completes in 3 to 5 minutes via high-concurrency cloud GPU rendering.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I see my past generations?" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    All past generations are permanently stored in [Generation History](/history/overview). You can search by SKU, filter by date or quality score, inspect pipeline flow graphs, and re-download full-resolution assets anytime at zero additional credit cost.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
