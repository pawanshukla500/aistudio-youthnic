# Product Images & References FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many product images should I upload per photoshoot?" icon="images">
    - **Minimum**: Exactly **1 front photo** is mandatory.
    - **Recommended Best Practice**: **2 to 3 photos** (1 Front view, 1 Back view, and 1 Macro Fabric / Swatch shot).
    - **Saree Shoots**: 3 to 4 dedicated component photos (Pallu, Pleats, Blouse, and Border) for best drape fidelity.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which product image should be used as the front reference?" icon="camera">
    Upload the clean, uncropped photo showing the garment's full front silhouette, neckline cut, front placket buttons, and hemline drop. This is assigned the `front` reference role and acts as the master anchor for the digital garment identity.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is a back image strictly required?" icon="arrow-rotate-left">
    **No, it is not strictly mandatory.** If you omit a back image, Youthnic AI Studio intelligently infers the rear construction based on the garment category and front silhouette. However, for garments with intricate back details—such as deep back cuts, latkan tassels, back zippers, or rear yoke embroidery—uploading a true back reference is strongly recommended to guarantee 100% rear accuracy.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I upload smartphone photos taken in my office?" icon="mobile-screen">
    **Yes!** Modern smartphone cameras (iPhone 12+, Samsung Galaxy S21+, Google Pixel) capture more than enough optical resolution. Simply ensure:
    - You shoot against a neutral, plain wall or seamless white foam board.
    - The room has even daylight without heavy yellow tungsten lamp casts.
    - The garment is steamed or ironed to remove transit creases.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between a Style Reference and a Product Reference?" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    - **Product Reference (`front`, `back`, `fabric_pattern`)**: Preserved with 100% fidelity as the garment worn by the model.
    - **Style Reference (`style_reference`)**: Used strictly to transfer lighting atmosphere, camera focal length, and architectural backdrop mood. The garment inside a style reference is **never** copied.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
