# Create a Plan

A **Plan** serves as a container for an entire seasonal drop, product capsule, or marketing push. It groups multiple catalog campaigns under a unified budget and delivery timeline.

---

## 1. Initializing the Plan Wizard

From the Planning overview, click the **+ Create Plan** button:

[SCREENSHOT REQUIRED:
Page: Planning (/planning)
Exact State: 'Create New Production Plan' modal open with step 1 'General Information' active.
Exact UI Section: Create Plan modal wizard.
Recommended Crop: 750x600
Recommended Dimensions: 750x600
Filename: planning-create-modal-step1.webp]

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan Title & Season Code">
    Enter a name for the plan (e.g. *"Festive Luxury Pret 2026"*) and a short season code (e.g. `FL-2026`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Planning Scope & Archetype">
    Choose the scope:
    - **Seasonal Collection Drop**: Multi-category collection (e.g. sarees, suits, lehengas).
    - **Event-Driven Capsule**: Dedicated launch tied to a specific festive or marketplace date.
    - **Continuous Replenishment / Core Catalog**: Evergreen daily photoshoot production.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Target SKU Count & Budget">
    Specify how many unique SKUs are planned for this drop (e.g. `50 SKUs`). The wizard calculates the estimated credit requirement (`50 × 5 poses = 250 Credits`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Key Milestone Dates">
    Define the timeline:
    - **Sampling Cutoff Date**: When physical garments must arrive.
    - **Imaging Production Window**: When AI photoshoots will be generated.
    - **Final Catalog Sign-Off**: Merchandiser approval deadline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- **Plan Owner / Lead Merchandiser**: Manages SKU scope and final sign-off.
- **Styling Lead**: Approves model choices, jewelry, and backdrops.
- **Production Operators**: Responsible for uploading photos and running Studio generations.

---

## 3. Plan Workspace Initialization

Upon clicking **Create Plan**, your plan enters the **Draft** state. You are directed to the Plan Workspace where you can begin structuring products, attaching catalog campaigns, and assigning sample tracking codes.
