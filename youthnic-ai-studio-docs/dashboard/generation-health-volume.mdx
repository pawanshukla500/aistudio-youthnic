- **Anatomical Coherence**: Unnatural finger flexion or facial asymmetry.
- **Colorimetry Deviation ($\Delta E > 6.0$)**: Garment hue shifted due to high-contrast warm studio lighting.
- **Silhouette Distortion**: Ruffle flares or hemline drape not matching reference photo geometry.
