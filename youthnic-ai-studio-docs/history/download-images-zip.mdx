# Download Images & ZIP Archives

All assets generated across your workspace are stored on high-speed cloud CDN infrastructure with permanent retention. You can re-download images or entire photoshoot ZIPs at any point in the future.

---

## 1. Single Photoshoot ZIP Export

From the History list or inside any individual generation detail view:

[SCREENSHOT REQUIRED:
Page: Generation History (/history)
Exact State: History card hover state showing 'Download ZIP' button and resolution dropdown.
Exact UI Section: Quick export button on history card.
Recommended Crop: 400x200
Recommended Dimensions: 400x200
Filename: history-card-download-zip.webp]

Click **Download ZIP** to immediately trigger browser download of the standard 5-pose archive:
- Includes all 5 poses named by SKU.
- Includes `manifest.json` with color hex codes and prompt parameters.
- Free of charge (0 credits consumed).

## 2. Multi-Photoshoot Bulk Compilation

If your e-commerce manager needs to download assets for 20 different styles generated over the past month:

[SCREENSHOT REQUIRED:
Page: Generation History (/history)
Exact State: Checkbox mode active in History. 12 photoshoots selected. Bottom floating action bar displays 'Export 12 Selected Photoshoots (60 Images) as Master ZIP'.
Exact UI Section: History bulk export action bar.
Recommended Crop: 950x160
Recommended Dimensions: 950x160
Filename: history-bulk-selection-export.webp]

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Checkboxes">
    Select the checkboxes on the target generation cards, or click **Select All in View**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Master ZIP Export">
    Click **Export Selected (.ZIP)** on the floating bottom bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Naming Preset">
    Select folder nesting syntax:
    - `Group by SKU Folder` (Subfolder for each SKU)
    - `Flat Listing Folder` (All files in root, prefixed with SKU)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download Archive">
    Youthnic's backend compiles the multi-gigabyte ZIP in seconds and streams it to your browser.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Direct CDN Links for E-Commerce Feeds

You do not need to download and manually re-upload images to Shopify or WooCommerce. Every image in History has a public, high-speed CDN URL:
- Click the **Copy CDN Link** icon on any image thumbnail.
- Paste the URL directly into your Shopify CSV, Magento feed, or Amazon flat file upload spreadsheet.
