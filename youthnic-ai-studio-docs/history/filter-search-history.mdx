# Filter & Search History

As your brand produces thousands of fashion photos across multiple seasons, finding a specific past shoot quickly is essential. Youthnic AI Studio provides instant multi-faceted search and filtering tools.

---

- **Product Title**: e.g., *"Emerald Velvet Bridal Lehenga"*

## Sorting Your Archive

Use the sort dropdown to order results by:
- **Newest First (Default)**: Chronological order of rendering.
- **Highest Quality Score**: Shows top-rated visual assets first.
- **SKU Code (A–Z)**: Alphabetical catalog order.
- **Credit Cost**: Identifies high-complexity shoots with multiple regenerations.
