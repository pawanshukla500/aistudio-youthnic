# Troubleshooting Overview

Encountering an unexpected error during image upload, garment analysis, or photoshoot generation can disrupt production deadlines. This section provides structured diagnostic guides for identifying causes and applying immediate fixes.

---

## Standard Troubleshooting Format

Every troubleshooting guide in this section is structured around four clear diagnostic steps:
1. **Problem**: What symptoms are visible in the UI or generation canvas.
2. **Possible Cause**: The technical or optical reason behind the failure.
3. **How to Fix**: Step-by-step actions you can take immediately to resolve the issue.
4. **When to Contact Administrator**: Circumstances requiring IT or workspace admin intervention.

## Immediate Emergency Checklist

If you encounter unexpected behavior:
- **Refresh Session**: Press `Ctrl + Shift + R` (`Cmd + Shift + R` on Mac) to clear stale cached state. Your session will automatically restore via local auto-save.
- **Inspect System Health**: Check [status.youthnic.com](https://status.youthnic.com) or the [System Health](/admin/system-health) console to verify whether cloud workers are operational.
- **Verify Credits**: Ensure your organization has not exhausted its monthly credit quota.
