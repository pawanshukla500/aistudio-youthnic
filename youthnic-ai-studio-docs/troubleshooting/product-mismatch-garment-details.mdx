# Product Mismatch & Garment Details Lost

---

## Problem

The generated image renders a complete fashion model, but the physical garment details do not match the real product:
- **Color Shift**: A navy blue garment is rendered as royal electric blue, or emerald green looks olive.
- **Altered Neckline**: A distinct V-neck or sweetheart neckline is rendered as a standard round neck.
- **Lost Embroidery / Zari**: Micro-gotapatti borders, sequins, or floral prints are smudged or omitted entirely.
- **Incorrect Drape / Silhouette**: An A-line kurti is rendered as a bodycon dress.

## Possible Causes

1. **Ambient Lighting Conflict**: Selecting a heavy *"Golden Hour"* or high-contrast dramatic sunset lighting preset casts warm yellow light across the entire image, shifting garment hues.
2. **Missing Fabric / Detail Reference**: Intricate embroidery was only visible in a distant full-body shot without a dedicated macro close-up.
3. **Product Identity Profile Unverified**: The AI vision analysis misclassified the fabric or neckline, and the user did not correct it before clicking generate.
4. **Pose 4 Seated vs. Walking Ambiguity**: The style reference contained a seated lifestyle setting, causing Pose 4 to render seated when an active walking hemline flare was desired.

---

## How to Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Lock the Exact Hex Code">
    Open the **Product Identity Profile** card. Click the **Primary Color Swatch** and enter the exact sRGB hex code from your physical fabric swatch (e.g. `#5B1E31`). Click the **Padlock Icon** to enforce strict color adherence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to High-Key Neutral Lighting">
    Change the Creative Direction lighting preset to **High-Key Commercial Studio** or **Soft North Daylight**. Neutral 5500K daylight ensures true color rendition on marketplace feeds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a Dedicated Macro Swatch">
    Drop a high-resolution 1:1 macro photo of the embroidery into the **Fabric / Pattern Reference** slot (`fabric_pattern`). This forces the attention layers to focus on stitch-level texture.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explicitly Direct Pose 4">
    If Pose 4 rendered as a seated shot and you wanted a walking shot: click **Edit Pose 4**, change the posture setting from `Auto-Detect` to `Force Walking Motion`, and regenerate that single pose for 1 credit.
  </Step>
</Steps>
