# Image Upload & Unsupported Files

---

## Problem

When dragging and dropping image files into the Studio or Catalog upload areas:
- The upload drops fail silently, or a red error alert appears: *"File Type Not Supported"*, *"File Exceeds 25MB Limit"*, or *"Invalid Image Dimension"*.
- The upload tile remains blank, or a broken image placeholder icon is displayed.

## Possible Causes

1. **Unsupported Proprietary Container Formats**: You uploaded an Apple Live Photo container (`.heic`, `.heif`), camera raw format (`.cr2`, `.nef`, `.arw`), or a PDF document.
2. **File Size Greater Than 25 MB**: Studio enforces a 25 MB per-file limit to ensure rapid browser uploads and worker processing.
3. **Corrupted EXIF or CMYK Color Profile**: Images exported in CMYK (print color space) rather than sRGB (web color space) cannot be processed by web browser canvases.
4. **Resolution Out of Bounds**: The image is larger than 8192 x 8192 px or smaller than 400 x 400 px.

---

## How to Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Convert HEIC / RAW to PNG or JPEG">
    - **Mac**: Open the photo in Preview, choose **File > Export**, and select **PNG** or **JPEG**.
    - **Windows**: Use the Photos app or an online image converter to save as sRGB JPEG.
    - **iPhone Settings**: To prevent future HEIC uploads, navigate on your phone to **Settings > Camera > Formats** and select **Most Compatible** (forces standard JPEG).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resize Files Exceeding 25 MB">
    If you have ultra-high-resolution RAW camera exports exceeding 25 MB, resize them to 3000 x 4000 px at 90% JPEG quality. This provides more than enough detail for AI vision conditioning while keeping file size under 6 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ensure sRGB Color Space">
    Ensure your graphics software (Photoshop / Lightroom) exports using the **sRGB IEC61966-2.1** color profile rather than Adobe RGB or CMYK.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## When to Contact Administrator

Contact your administrator if:
- Standard PNG or JPEG files under 5 MB fail with network errors like `413 PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE` or `502 BAD_GATEWAY`, which indicates an upstream corporate firewall or proxy issue.
