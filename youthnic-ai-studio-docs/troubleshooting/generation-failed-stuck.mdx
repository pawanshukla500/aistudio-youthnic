# Generation Failed or Stuck

---

## Problem

After clicking **Generate Photoshoot**:
- One or more pose tiles remain stuck in the `Queued` or `Generating (0%)` state for longer than 3 minutes.
- A red error banner appears on the tile: *"Generation Failed: GPU Worker Timeout (504)"* or *"Worker Interrupted"*.
- The generation timer continues counting up past 180 seconds without producing pixels.

## Possible Causes

1. **Temporary Cloud GPU Cluster Spike**: Heavy global traffic or provider autoscaling delays during peak hours.
2. **WebSocket / Network Disconnect**: Your local internet connection experienced a micro-drop, causing the browser to lose the live WebSocket event stream from the worker.
3. **Complex Multi-Layer Conditioning Conflict**: Uploading contradictory references (e.g. tagging an image as both a `front` garment and a `style_reference`) that causes the latent sampler to fail convergence.
4. **Safety Filter Trigger**: A reference photo inadvertently triggered automated safety guardrails.

---

## How to Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Browser Network Connection">
    Check the small connection status dot in the Studio footer. If it shows amber or red, refresh the browser page (`Ctrl + R` / `Cmd + R`). Your session state will reload automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Retry Failed Pose'">
    Hover over the failed or stuck tile and click the circular **Retry** icon. This drops the stale worker task and assigns the render to a fresh GPU node.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Credit Refund">
    Check your credit counter in the top bar. Youthnic automatically refunds credits for any failed or timed-out pose. If a pose failed, 1 credit was immediately returned to your balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Prompt & Reference Roles">
    Verify that your reference photos have clean semantic roles assigned. Avoid uploading identical images to multiple conflicting slots.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## When to Contact Administrator

Contact your administrator or support if:
- All generations fail consistently across all users in your organization workspace.
- The error displays `INSUFFICIENT_ORGANIZATION_CREDITS` or `PROVIDER_CIRCUIT_BREAKER_TRIPPED`.
