# Failed Quality Validation Check

---

## Problem

After generation completes:
- An image tile displays an amber warning badge `Needs Review` (Score 70–84) or a red badge `Defective` (Score under 70).
- The image exhibits anatomical anomalies (e.g. six fingers on one hand, unnatural wrist curvature, asymmetrical eyes) or severe garment edge fringing.

## Possible Causes

1. **Complex Hand-to-Body Occlusion**: Poses where the model rests her hand inside a draped lehenga pocket or grasps a sheer organza dupatta fold can cause finger keypoint ambiguity during diffusion.
2. **Extreme Perspective / Low Angle Distortion**: Extreme wide-angle camera framing that stretches peripheral limbs.
3. **Artifact Bleed from Low-Res Reference**: Heavy compression noise in the source photo propagating into the generated model's skin texture.

---

## How to Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the QA Badge to Inspect Defects">
    Click the red or amber badge on the image tile. The **QA Diagnostic Popover** highlights the exact defective zone with a bounding box (e.g. *"Right hand keypoint score: 58/100"*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Quick Inpaint / Face & Hand Fix">
    Click the **Fix Anatomy** icon on the tile hover menu. The system runs an isolated 4-second latent inpainting pass targeted strictly at the hand or face, correcting anatomy without altering the garment or background.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Regenerate with Directed Prompt Feedback">
    If inpainting is insufficient, click **Regenerate**, choose **Directed Revision**, and type: *"Model hands relaxed naturally by side, clear five fingers visible"*. Re-render for 1 credit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manual Approval Override">
    If the QA engine flagged an artistic background shadow as a defect but the model and garment look commercially flawless to your eye, click **Approve Override** to mark the image as ready for export.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## When to Contact Administrator

Contact your administrator if:
- More than 15% of all generated images are scoring below 70 across different garments, suggesting a need to recalibrate the QA vision model sensitivity threshold in [Admin Settings](/admin/ai-routing-models).
