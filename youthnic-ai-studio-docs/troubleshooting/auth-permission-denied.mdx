# Authentication & Permission Denied

---

## Problem

When navigating within Youthnic AI Studio or clicking an action button:
- A full-screen or toast error appears: *"403 Forbidden: You do not have permission to perform this action"* or *"Access Denied"*.
- The browser repeatedly redirects back to the login page (`/login`) despite entering valid credentials.
- Action buttons (like **Generate Photoshoot** or **Approve Campaign**) appear disabled with a padlock tooltip.

## Possible Causes

1. **Expired Session Token**: Authentication JWTs expire automatically after 7 days of inactivity for enterprise security compliance.
2. **Insufficient RBAC Role**: Your user account is assigned the `Read-Only Viewer` role, which permits inspecting images and downloading files but restricts rendering.
3. **Department Team Isolation**: You are attempting to access a catalog campaign belonging to another department team (e.g. Western team member opening a Bridal campaign) where team privacy is enforced.
4. **Browser Third-Party Cookie / Local Storage Blocking**: Strict ad-blockers or privacy extensions blocking the Supabase authentication cookie.

---

## How to Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hard Sign Out & Re-Login">
    Click your user avatar in the bottom left corner, select **Sign Out**, and log back in. This requests a fresh authentication token with updated role permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disable Aggressive Privacy Extensions">
    Ensure ad-blockers (such as uBlock Origin or Privacy Badger) have `*.youthnic.com` whitelisted so authentication tokens can persist in local storage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Current Role">
    Navigate to your profile settings. Check your assigned role: if you are an active catalog operator but your role displays *Viewer*, request a role update from your administrator.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## When to Contact Administrator

Contact your workspace administrator if:
- You need your account upgraded from `Viewer` to `Studio Operator` or `Lead Merchandiser`.
- You were recently added to a new department team but cannot view their campaigns.
- Your account is locked due to repeated incorrect password attempts.
