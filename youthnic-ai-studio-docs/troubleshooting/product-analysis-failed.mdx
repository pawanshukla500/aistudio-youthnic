# Product Analysis Failed

---

## Problem

When clicking **Analyze Garment** in the Studio or running **Batch Preflight** in Catalog Production:
- The progress spinner runs for 15+ seconds and then displays a red error badge: *"Garment Vision Analysis Failed: Optical Parser Timeout"* or *"Unable to Detect Primary Garment Silhouette"*.
- The **Product Identity Profile** remains empty or displays question mark badges `?` across fabric, color, and neckline fields.

## Possible Causes

1. **Severe Motion Blur or Low Resolution**: The uploaded photo is under 800px wide or exhibits heavy lens blur, preventing the optical model from identifying seam lines or fabric weave.
2. **Cluttered or Distracting Background**: The garment photo was shot in a chaotic factory setting with other clothing piles, machinery, or complex patterns in the frame that confuse foreground separation.
3. **Extreme Low-Light or Underexposure**: The photo is so dark that shadow clipping obscures whether the garment is navy, black, or deep purple.
4. **Unsupported Role Mapping**: No image was assigned the mandatory `Front` reference role.

---

## How to Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify Role Assignment">
    Ensure at least one uploaded photo has the dropdown selector set strictly to **Front Reference**. If only `fabric_pattern` or `style_reference` is uploaded, analysis cannot determine the garment silhouette.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Crop Extraneous Background Clutter">
    If the garment was photographed against a busy factory background, crop the image tightly around the garment before uploading, or use our automated background remover tool in the upload card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ensure Even White Balance">
    Upload a photo captured under neutral, diffused light where the true fabric color and neckline cut are clearly visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Retry Analysis'">
    Click **Analyze Garment** again. If the issue was a temporary vision API timeout, the second pass will typically succeed within 4–6 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manual Attribute Fallback">
    If you cannot re-shoot the garment immediately, click **Manual Override** inside the Product Identity card and manually select the silhouette, fabric type, and color hex code from the taxonomy dropdowns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## When to Contact Administrator

Contact your workspace administrator or Youthnic technical support if:
- Analysis fails repeatedly across multiple different high-resolution photos that previously worked.
- The error message displays `500 INTERNAL_VISION_ROUTER_ERROR` or `UPSTREAM_QUOTA_EXHAUSTED`.
