# Catalog Colourway Incomplete & Preflight Errors

---

## Problem

When attempting to click **Generate Catalog** or **Schedule Generation** inside a catalog campaign:
- The generate button is grayed out or displays an error banner: *"Batch Generation Blocked: 4 Colourways Have Unresolved Preflight Errors"*.
- Certain colourway cards display red warning icons and cannot be selected.

## Possible Causes

1. **Missing Mandatory Front Reference**: One or more colourway cards do not have a front garment photo uploaded.
2. **Severe Resolution Rejection**: An uploaded photo is under 800px wide, failing the minimum optical preflight threshold.
3. **Color Dye-Lot Swatch Conflict**: An uploaded photo has a primary optical color that drastically contradicts the SKU's assigned color name (e.g. uploaded photo is bright red, but SKU is labeled *"Forest Green"*).
4. **Pending Creative Lead Approval**: The campaign requires creative sign-off, which has not yet been granted.

---

## How to Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter by Incomplete Cards">
    Click the **Incomplete** pill on the filter toolbar to isolate the defective colourways.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Missing Reference Photos">
    Drag and drop the missing front or back photo onto the highlighted dropzones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update Conflicting Color Names">
    If the garment photo is correct but the SKU color tag was mistyped, edit the colourway label to match the true garment shade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute Partial Batch (Workaround)">
    If physical sample photos for the remaining 4 colourways will not arrive until tomorrow: check the boxes only for the **Ready** colourways and click **Generate Selected**. This renders all ready SKUs immediately without waiting for the incomplete cards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## When to Contact Administrator

Contact your administrator if:
- All cards display green checkmarks, but generation remains locked due to an unapproved approval gate where the designated approver is unavailable. An admin can grant emergency sign-off.
