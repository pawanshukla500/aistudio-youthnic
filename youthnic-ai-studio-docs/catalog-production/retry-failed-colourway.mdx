# Retry Failed Colourway

In a batch of 50 colourways, 48 may render flawlessly while 2 encounter an upstream GPU timeout or fail automated anatomy validation. Youthnic AI Studio ensures you never have to re-render the successful 48 items to fix the remaining 2.

```mermaid
graph TD
    A[Batch of 50 Colourways] --> B[48 Completed & Validated]
    A --> C[2 Flagged / Failed]
    C --> D[Isolate Failed Colourway Cards]
    D --> E[Inspect Error Log & QA Defect]
    E --> F[Click 'Retry Colourway' (0 Extra Credits for Failures)]
    F --> G[Re-render & Join Completed Batch]
```

---

## Diagnosing Colourway Failure

When a colourway encounters an error, its card on the production board highlights in red with an **Inspection Button**:

Click **Inspect Failure** to view the diagnostic log:
- **Upstream Worker Timeout**: Temporary cloud GPU disconnect. Automatically retriable.
- **Garment Delta-E Mismatch**: The generated garment color deviated beyond tolerance from the reference photo.
- **Anatomy / Pose Coherence**: Detected flawed hand geometry or face distortion.

<Tip>
If an image fails due to system error or automated QA rejection, **zero credits are charged**. The unused credits are automatically credited back to your account.
</Tip>

## Retrying the Colourway

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Error Drawer">
    Click the **Retry** icon on the failed colourway card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review or Adjust Conditioning">
    If the failure was caused by optical blur in the back photo, you can replace the back photo right inside the modal without leaving the catalog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Alternate AI Route (Optional)">
    You can optionally toggle **Route to Fallback AI Provider** to dispatch the job to a secondary model cluster.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Retry Now">
    The single colourway re-enters the worker queue. Once rendered, it automatically merges into the completed catalog.
  </Step>
</Steps>
