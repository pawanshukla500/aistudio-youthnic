# Add Custom Event

While Youthnic AI Studio provides comprehensive coverage of public festivals and marketplace sales, brands frequently have their own internal product milestones—such as a *"Spring Bridal Drop"*, a *"Resort Capsule Launch"*, or a *"Celebrity Collaboration Reveal"*.

You can add custom events to synchronize your internal deadlines with AI catalog production.

---

## Creating a Custom Event

Click the **+ Add Custom Event** button in the top right corner of the Events interface:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Event Name & Description">
    Enter a clear descriptive title (e.g. *"Summer Pret '27 Collection Drop"*) and internal campaign notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Category Assignment">
    Choose the classification:
    - **Brand Product Launch**
    - **Influencer / PR Campaign**
    - **Flash Sale / Pop-Up**
    - **Internal Catalog Deadline**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Date Range & Launch Day">
    Select the public launch date or date window when the collection goes live on your storefront.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Production Lead Time">
    Specify how many days before launch the photoshoot assets must be completed (Default: `30 days`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Target Region & Categories">
    Tag applicable states and primary garment silhouettes (e.g. *Western Wear > Maxi Dresses*).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Visibility & Sharing

By default, custom events are visible to all members of your organization workspace. If you wish to restrict visibility to specific merchandising teams:
- Under **Access Permissions**, toggle between **Entire Organization** or **Private to Team** (e.g. *Bridal Design Team*).

---

## Editing & Managing Custom Events

- To edit dates or details, click on the custom event chip and select **Edit Event**.
- To remove an event, click the **Delete** trash icon in the event detail drawer. Note that deleting a custom event does not delete any catalog campaigns that were previously planned from it.
