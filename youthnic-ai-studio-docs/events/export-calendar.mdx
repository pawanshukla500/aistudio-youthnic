# Export & Share Calendar

Merchandising teams, factory sample managers, and marketing agencies often need access to the event calendar in external tools like Excel, Google Calendar, Notion, or Asana.

Youthnic AI Studio provides multi-format export and sharing capabilities.

### Export Columns Included

- `Event ID` & `Event Title`
- `Category` (Festival / Marketplace Sale / Regional Holiday / Custom)
- `Start Date` & `End Date`
- `States / Regions Covered`
- `Recommended Lead Time (Days)`
- `Production Deadline Date`
- `Associated Catalog Campaigns`

## 2. Sync with Google Calendar & Outlook (iCal)

Subscribe to real-time calendar updates directly inside Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Microsoft Outlook:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click iCal Subscription">
    Select **Sync with iCal / Google Calendar** from the Export menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy Secure Feed URL">
    Copy your workspace's private webcal URL (`webcal://aistudio.youthnic.com/api/v1/events/calendar.ics?token=...`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subscribe in Calendar Client">
    In Google Calendar, click **Other Calendars > From URL**, paste the link, and subscribe. Newly added festivals and custom brand milestones will appear on your personal calendar automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Email Calendar Digest

Schedule an automated weekly or monthly calendar digest:
- **Recipients**: Add team member email addresses or distribution lists (e.g. `merchandising@yourbrand.com`).
- **Cadence**: Every Monday at 9:00 AM.
- **Content**: Highlights all events occurring in the next 30 days and alerts the team to upcoming catalog production deadlines.
