# Release Notes

Stay up to date with new features, model upgrades, and workflow improvements introduced across Youthnic AI Studio.

---

## September 2026 — v2.6 Release

### Studio & Vision Conditioning
- **Pose 6 Is Now Chosen Per Product**: The sixth frame no longer follows a fixed per-garment template. During analysis the vision model inspects the product references, picks the single feature a buyer judges the product by — a yoke, neckline, sleeve, print, the cut and flare of the bottom wear, a hem or border, a dupatta, a saree's pallu or drape — and designs the frame around it, recording the decision, the shot type and the reasoning.
- **Enforced Non-Duplication**: The sixth frame may not be a second hero shot, and may not re-shoot the Pose 5 close-up subject. When the strongest feature is already Pose 5's subject, the frame is automatically widened to show how that feature sits on the worn garment instead of re-cropping it.
- **Showcase Feedback Loop**: Approvals, rejections, regeneration requests and QA refusals on the sixth frame are now scored per category, garment family, feature region and shot type, and fed back into later analyses as house taste that product references still outrank.

## September 2026 — v2.5 Release

### Studio & Vision Conditioning
- **Six-Pose Catalog Plan**: Added a sixth, garment-led **Showcase** frame to every shoot. Sarees get a drape-led pallu frame, kurti/kurta sets get a head-to-toe frame proving top and bottom together, short kurtis and tops get a playful moment against the shoot's own backdrop, and standalone long kurtis and dresses get a full-length fall-and-fit frame.
- **Garment-Aware Pose Grammar**: Pose direction is now selected from the detected garment family instead of a single generic ethnic-wear prior. Bengali sarees (tant, jamdani, garad, korial, baluchari, lal-paar, aatpoure drape) hold an upright serene posture with the aanchal placed on its recorded shoulder and its artwork and border readable; other traditional sarees follow the recorded drape plan; kurti sets prove fit, length and sleeve shape; short kurtis and tops move naturally with the backdrop.
- **Configurable Bottom Wear**: A new **Bottom wear** output setting (`Auto`, `Outfit includes bottom wear`, `Top only`) decides how a shoot presents a bottom garment. Top-only shoots are explicitly prohibited from inventing coordinated matching bottoms, and automatic QA follows the same setting.
- **Scoped Fidelity Locks**: Each shoot now carries only the bottom-wear substitution guard that matches its recorded cut, so the instruction that applies is no longer diluted by five that do not.

### Performance
- **Parallel Reference Loading**: Product, style and model references are fetched concurrently instead of one at a time.
- **Pose-Scoped Reference Loading**: Each pose downloads only the references its prompt can actually use, with a full-set fallback when an optional asset is unavailable.
- **Overlapped Pre-flight**: The budget check, the Pose 1 continuity lookup, the approved-learning read and the seeded fashion-knowledge read now run alongside reference loading instead of adding serial round trips to every paid attempt.

## September 2026 — v2.4 Release

### Catalog Production
- **Multi-SKU Smart-Match Ingestion**: Upload folders of hundreds of garment photos; filenames like `SKU-001_front.jpg` now automatically map to their respective colourway cards.
- **Sequential Overnight Generation**: Added a dedicated queue mode that renders colourway-by-colourway during off-peak hours with automated failure pauses.
- **Master Manifest CSV Export**: Bulk catalog ZIP downloads now include a listing manifest spreadsheet pre-populated with public CDN image links, color hexes, and detected fabric composition for easy Shopify, Amazon, and Myntra uploads.

### Studio & Vision Conditioning
- **Enhanced Pose 4 Context-Aware Logic**: Improved editorial pose synthesis to automatically detect lifestyle style references and render poised seated poses on authentic sandstone settees when appropriate.
- **Saree Component Decoupling**: Upgraded Saree Mode to decouple pallu, pleat, and blouse reference embeddings, eliminating pattern bleed across vertical drape gathers.
- **Unobstructed Rear Pose Prompt Rule**: Pose 3 now strictly pins dupattas and stoles forward over the left shoulder, guaranteeing zero obstruction of back neck embroidery and tassel ties.

### Dashboard & Analytics
- **14-Day Rolling Analytics**: Visual charts tracking daily image volume, quality score trajectory, and hourly activity heatmaps.
- **Real-Time Token Telemetry**: Full transparency into vision tokens, prompt synthesis tokens, and diffusion latency steps.

## July 2026 — v2.2 Release

### Studio
- **Five-Pose E-Commerce Standard**: Standardized the 5-shot catalog plan (Front Hero, Three-Quarter, Rear View, Editorial, Detail Close-Up) with one-click aspect ratio presets for Myntra (4:5), Nykaa (3:4), and Amazon (4:5).
- **Automated Quality Assurance Engine**: Introduced real-time optical scoring across anatomical hand/finger counts, facial symmetry, and delta-E colorimetric adherence.
- **Single-Pose Regeneration**: Re-render individual poses for 1 credit while preserving the other 4 completed images on the canvas.

### Core Platform
- **4K Studio Lightbox**: Pan and 400% zoom loupe inspection for micro-embroidery, gotapatti borders, and sequin textures.
- **Automated Credit Refund System**: Immediate 100% refund of credits if a GPU worker times out or an image fails quality validation.
