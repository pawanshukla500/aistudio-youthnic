## November 2026 — v3.0 Release

### Agentic LangGraph + Product DNA V2 & Medium-Reasoning Vision Routing
- **Default Meta Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor (`medium` Thinking) + OpenAI GPT 6.1 Terra (`medium`) Fallback**: Product Truth analysis and 6-pose planning default to **medium thinking** on **Meta Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor** (`muse-spark-1.3-contributor`, with sibling fallback to `1.2-contributor`) and **OpenAI GPT 6.1 Terra** (`gpt-6.1-terra:medium`), balancing deep garment geometry, drape, and styling accuracy with reliable latency inside the 180s gateway budget (saved policies in Administration → AI models take precedence, `high` remains selectable, and emergency `< 60s` hop budgets clamp reasoning to `low`).
- **Flagship Image Generation Default (`gpt-image-2.6-sunburst`)**: Verified and defaulted Studio, Catalog, and newly enrolled seller workspaces to `gpt-image-2.6-sunburst` (3:4, 2K `1536x2048`, `primary_reasoning = 'none'`), delivering sharper zari/zardozi weave reproduction than `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst`.
- **Cut-Isolated Farshi, Gen-Z Saree & Kurti Set Intelligence**: Isolated Farshi salwar two-leg gathers, waist-side narrowing, and 4-inch upper-hem taper strictly to Farshi SKUs; upgraded Gen-Z saree draping and top-only vs. multi-piece kurti set pose choreography.
- **LangGraph + FastAPI + Postgres Checkpointing & Valkey Caching**: Deployed Python FastAPI LangGraph multi-agent orchestration with Postgres checkpoints (`PostgresSaver`), immutable SHA-256 Product DNA V2 versions (`dna_versions`), Valkey caching & Celery brokerage, and background workers alongside the Edge API.

### Approved Product DNA Shapes Generation
- **Approve Once, Applied Every Shoot**: A Product DNA version you approve in Studio now adds its invariants and prohibited changes to the image prompt and the product consistency check of every later shoot of that SKU. Unused background services (a separate task worker and an unused memory search) were removed; generation and checking run in one place.

### Reference-Led Pose & Expression Planning
- **Pose 4 and Pose 6 Match the Style Reference Posture**: When the person in the style reference sits, leans, kneels or strikes a signature pose and the posture suits the product, Pose 4 matches it and Pose 6 can use a variation with a different angle and framing. Poses 1, 2, 3 and 5 stay catalog frames. Product guards keep the pose product-led for Farshi bottom wear with a folded posture, reclining sarees, frozen walking strides, tight showcase crops and full-length showcase features, and a sit request in the product notes comes first for Pose 4. The reference outfit, face, hair and handheld objects are never copied.
- **Expression Planning**: Each shoot gets one planned attitude, energy, gaze and smile level, chosen from the style reference and the product, and every frame adapts it. Forced or toothy grins are never requested.
- **Always-On Product Consistency Check**: Every pose is checked for shape, colour, material, logo and details against the product photos before it is accepted. A failed image is saved to the rejected archive instead of being deleted (the upload is retried once; if storage is unavailable the archive copy is skipped and logged), and the pose is regenerated within the retry limit; if the last attempt fails only this check (automatic QA, when on, passed it), that image is kept for human review. Full automatic QA stays optional.
- **Learning From Outcomes**: Matched and product-led pose decisions are scored by the consistency check and by reviewer approvals per product type, and later shoots prefer what passed.
- **Batch Regeneration Runs on the Server**: Regenerating several poses from History now queues all of them in one step. They run in pose order, so a regenerated Pose 1 finishes first and anchors the face, set and props of the rest. If Pose 1 cannot be produced, the other selected poses keep their current images. You no longer need to keep the window open.
- **Requested Props Keep the Full Consistency Rules**: When the product notes ask the model to hold a prop (for example a coffee glass and a bag), every frame still carries the rule that only the pose, camera angle and framing change. The hand directions and the styling list no longer contradict the requested props. Every frame carries the same props Pose 1 carries, and a bag or drink that only appears in a product photo is never used.
- **Natural, Varied Expressions in Every Frame**: Each frame where the face shows now has its own gaze, head angle, expression and hand gesture. Pose 1 meets the lens, Pose 2 looks just past it over the turn, Pose 4 looks away into the set, Pose 5 turns a quarter and looks back, and Pose 6 looks toward the featured garment. The model no longer repeats one frozen smile and straight-ahead stare with both arms hanging. This applies to existing shoots when you regenerate them.
- **Pose 4 Follows the Reference Person's Look**: New analyses record how the style-reference person looks: their expression, gaze, head angle, hand gesture and camera angle. Pose 4 takes that look, with your model's face and your product unchanged. When Pose 6 copies the reference posture it keeps its own camera angle and look. To use it on an existing shoot, click **Clone Session**, then **Re-analyze Ground Truth** in the Studio (cloning alone keeps the old analysis).
- **Every Retry Gets a Fresh QA Verdict**: Automatic QA no longer reads its own earlier rejections when it judges a new attempt. Reading them could make it repeat a failure on an image that was already fixed.
- **Back View Keeps the Hair Off the Back**: The rear frame keeps the hair swept forward or up, so the back neckline and upper back stay visible for the consistency check.
- **Six Product-Aware Poses: Signature Reference and Product Highlight**: The six frames are now Full Front, Turning, Back, Signature Reference, Face + Details and Product Highlight. Pose 4 reproduces the style-reference person's pose, camera angle and framing (seated, leaning, standing or other) in your garment whenever the reference shows a person; it stays product-led only for a seated request when the reference is not seated, Farshi bottom wear with a folded posture, or a reclining saree, and a walking pose becomes a grounded weight shift. Pose 6 is now a tight close-up of the product's most distinctive feature, such as a back lace-up and its bow, sleeve cuffs, a dupatta, the hem or the print, cropped where that feature sits and never the Pose 5 subject; it uses the product photo that shows the feature best (the back photo for a back detail). The back view names everything that must match the uploaded back photo and never invents a back detail. To use it on an existing shoot, click **Clone Session**, then **Re-analyze Ground Truth** in the Studio.

### Studio & History
- **Clone Session Opens Ready to Generate**: A cloned shoot now opens in the Studio with its product photos, analysis and six-pose plan already loaded. It no longer opens empty asking you to upload the photos and analyse them again. Generate right away, or click **Re-analyze Ground Truth** when the Studio says the analysis is from an older version. The clone also keeps the original shoot's SKU code, so its images are named after that SKU and an approved Product DNA for it still applies.
- **One Analysis per Set of Photos**: An automatic analysis and a click on **Analyze References Now** (or a retry after a dropped connection) now share one analysis of the same photos instead of running and paying for two.
- **Slow Analyses Finish Instead of Failing**: When the primary analysis model is slow or times out, the analysis keeps running on the server and the configured fallback model gets a full window to finish. Before, it got about a minute and the analysis usually failed. The Studio keeps waiting (up to about ten minutes in the rare worst case) and shows the result when it is ready. Changing the photos still stops the old analysis.
- **Product DNA Verdict on Every Card**: With automatic QA off, each pose card in History shows the always-on product DNA check result (for example "Product DNA check passed · 95%") instead of an orange "Not QA-checked" bar.
- **Clearer Pose Re-generation in the Flow View**: The flow view says why **Re-generate** waits (type what to change first, because each version is a paid generation), and versions read "awaiting review" instead of "pending".
- **Redesigned Generation Flow Board**: **Inspect Pipeline Flow** now lays the shoot out in four groups, left to right: **Input** (product photos, model face, style reference), **Product analysis** (the analysis model, its fallbacks, the product facts it read and the six-pose plan), **Prompting** (each pose's full stored prompt, scrollable, with the photos it used) and **Output images**. Each output card shows the image with its product DNA or QA verdict, the photos that fed it, a change note with **Run** to queue a new version of that pose, its version history, and the model, aspect ratio, size and quality it ran with. The header shows the SKU, status, analysis and image models, settings, poses done and cost. The board opens fitted to the screen (on a phone it starts at the inputs) and loads with a placeholder layout instead of a spinner. A cloned or Studio-started shoot now shows its input photos instead of an empty input column.
- **Fewer Error Screens on a Busy Server**: Opening the flow board, loading a cloned shoot into the Studio and downloading a pose now retry once when the server is briefly busy, instead of showing an error.
- **Clearer Pose Progress in the Studio**: The live progress card names each pose as Queued, Generating, Done or Failed. A failed pose shows its error and a Retry button that re-queues that pose only once the shoot has finished, and a finished image opens full size with a link to the generation flow.
- **A Re-generating Pose Shows Its Own Stage**: Right after you re-generate a pose, the shoot's row in History reads "Pose 1 queued for re-generation" for that pose. It no longer shows the stage left by an earlier run, or a pose number higher than the shoot has.
- **QA Checks the Pose 2 Turn**: When automatic QA runs on Pose 2 (a shoot with QA on, or **Re-run QA Model** in History), it now tests the three-quarter turn itself. A frame that still faces the camera like Pose 1, or turns to a full side profile, fails; a turn of roughly 35 to 45 degrees passes, and the result states the turn it saw.
- **Smoother Pages While a Shoot Generates**: History, the Studio and Catalog check for progress every few seconds. When nothing has changed, the page no longer redraws, and an open shoot in History is not redrawn when you search, open a confirmation or another shoot updates.
- **Cleaner History Shoot Header**: An open shoot's header is now two quiet lines (format and models, then spent vs estimate and retries) and three buttons: **View references**, **Clone Session** and **Download ZIP**. **Pipeline details** opens the per-stage costs and retries, and **Download approved** appears only once poses are approved.
- **History Page Polish**: Older cloned shoots show their product name instead of an internal id, in the list, dialogs and downloaded file names. Rows and pose images open from the keyboard, and the progress and date show on phones. Pose actions (regenerate, download) are visible on touch screens, and hovering one pose no longer animates all six. Queued and failed poses say so on the image. Every History popup closes with Escape and stops the page behind it from scrolling; the pose preview shows the whole image on phones with the review panel below, and shows a spinner instead of the previous frame while the next one loads. A QA score that never ran reads "Off" instead of a red 0%, retries are counted correctly ("2 retries across 2 poses"), and the Pipeline Modes panel shows the real provider and fallback policy.

## September 2026 — v2.6 Release

### Studio & Vision Conditioning
- **Pose 6 Is Now Chosen Per Product**: The sixth frame no longer follows a fixed per-garment template. During analysis the vision model inspects the product references, picks the single feature a buyer judges the product by — a yoke, neckline, sleeve, print, the cut and flare of the bottom wear, a hem or border, a dupatta, a saree's pallu or drape — and designs the frame around it, recording the decision, the shot type and the reasoning.
- **Enforced Non-Duplication**: The sixth frame may not be a second hero shot, and may not re-shoot the Pose 5 close-up subject. When the strongest feature is already Pose 5's subject, the frame is automatically widened to show how that feature sits on the worn garment instead of re-cropping it.
- **Showcase Feedback Loop**: Approvals, rejections, regeneration requests and QA refusals on the sixth frame are now scored per category, garment family, feature region and shot type, and fed back into later analyses as house taste that product references still outrank.

## September 2026 — v2.5 Release

### Studio & Vision Conditioning
- **Six-Pose Catalog Plan**: Added a sixth, garment-led **Showcase** frame to every shoot. Sarees get a drape-led pallu frame, kurti/kurta sets get a head-to-toe frame proving top and bottom together, short kurtis and tops get a playful moment against the shoot's own backdrop, and standalone long kurtis and dresses get a full-length fall-and-fit frame.
- **Garment-Aware Pose Grammar**: Pose direction is now selected from the detected garment family instead of a single generic ethnic-wear prior. Bengali sarees (tant, jamdani, garad, korial, baluchari, lal-paar, aatpoure drape) hold an upright serene posture with the aanchal placed on its recorded shoulder and its artwork and border readable; other traditional sarees follow the recorded drape plan; kurti sets prove fit, length and sleeve shape; short kurtis and tops move naturally with the backdrop.
- **Configurable Bottom Wear**: A new **Bottom wear** output setting (`Auto`, `Outfit includes bottom wear`, `Top only`) decides how a shoot presents a bottom garment. Top-only shoots are explicitly prohibited from inventing coordinated matching bottoms, and automatic QA follows the same setting.
- **Scoped Fidelity Locks**: Each shoot now carries only the bottom-wear substitution guard that matches its recorded cut, so the instruction that applies is no longer diluted by five that do not.

### Performance
- **Parallel Reference Loading**: Product, style and model references are fetched concurrently instead of one at a time.
- **Pose-Scoped Reference Loading**: Each pose downloads only the references its prompt can actually use, with a full-set fallback when an optional asset is unavailable.
- **Overlapped Pre-flight**: The budget check, the Pose 1 continuity lookup, the approved-learning read and the seeded fashion-knowledge read now run alongside reference loading instead of adding serial round trips to every paid attempt.

- **Unobstructed Rear Pose Prompt Rule**: Pose 3 now strictly pins dupattas and stoles forward over the left shoulder, guaranteeing zero obstruction of back neck embroidery and tassel ties.

### Dashboard & Analytics
